# Behavioral Interview Questions

## BEH-001 — Cross-Functional Production Launch

### Main Question

Tell me about a time you coordinated with engineers and other stakeholders to launch an AI-enabled capability or software system into production. What was your responsibility, how did you align the people involved, and what did you do to address disagreements or risks?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. Which decision did you personally own, and how did you communicate it to the different audiences involved?
2. What did you do when a partner, customer, or internal team had a constraint that affected the launch plan?
3. Looking back, what would you change about the collaboration?

## BEH-002 — Explain an Agent Tradeoff

### Main Question

Tell me about a time you explained a complex AI-agent or software-architecture decision to a non-technical customer, leader, or partner. What tradeoff did you need to make understandable, and how did you confirm that the explanation supported a decision?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. How did you adapt the explanation for that audience without hiding important uncertainty or risk?
2. What questions or objections did you receive, and how did you respond?
3. How did the conversation affect the eventual design or delivery plan?

## BEH-003 — Unexpected AI Behavior

### Main Question

Tell me about a time an AI system, automated workflow, or production service behaved unexpectedly. How did you investigate what happened, decide what to do under pressure, and reduce the chance of recurrence?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. What evidence did you use to distinguish a model issue from a data, integration, or deterministic-code issue?
2. How did you communicate the impact and uncertainty while the investigation was still in progress?
3. What safeguard, test, monitoring, or process did you add afterward?

## BEH-004 — Raise the Team's AI Standard

### Main Question

Tell me about a time you helped another engineer or a team improve its approach to building, reviewing, testing, or operating an AI-enabled system. What did you do personally, and how did you make the improvement useful rather than merely prescriptive?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. How did you determine what guidance or support the person or team actually needed?
2. Describe a moment when you had to give difficult technical feedback. How did you do it respectfully?
3. How did you tell whether the new practice was understood and being applied?

## BEH-005 — Own a Production Failure

### Main Question

Tell me about a production failure or significant defect for which you were responsible or shared responsibility. How did you respond, what did you communicate, and what lasting improvement did you drive afterward?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. What did you do first to contain user or customer impact?
2. How did you separate accountability from assigning blame during the investigation?
3. What evidence showed that the corrective action addressed the underlying cause rather than only the symptom?

## BEH-006 — Draw an Autonomy Boundary

### Main Question

Tell me about a time you decided that an automated or AI-driven feature needed a safety boundary, human review, restricted permissions, or a different level of autonomy. What risk or uncertainty drove the decision, and how did you bring others along?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. How did you decide which actions could be automated and which required confirmation or escalation?
2. What did you do to make the system's behavior explainable, auditable, or reversible?
3. How did you revisit the boundary as you learned more from testing or real use?

## BEH-007 — Push for the Hard Right Choice

### Main Question

Tell me about a time you pushed for a technically sound, safer, or more valuable course of action when a stakeholder or much of the team preferred an easier path. What made the decision important, how did you make your case, and what did you do after the decision was made?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. What evidence did you bring, and how did you distinguish a material risk from a personal preference?
2. How did you remain constructive with people who disagreed with you?
3. If your recommendation was not accepted, how did you support the final decision and continue managing the risk?

## BEH-008 — Quality Under Deadline

### Main Question

Tell me about a time you had to deliver an important software or AI capability under a tight deadline while protecting quality, security, or reliability. How did you decide what could be reduced or deferred, what could not be compromised, and how did you communicate those choices?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. Which concrete quality bar or risk boundary did you refuse to relax, and why?
2. What validation, monitoring, rollback, or manual control did you use to make the reduced scope safe enough to ship?
3. What follow-through occurred after the deadline so temporary compromises did not become permanent debt?

## BEH-009 — Sudden Requirement Change

### Main Question

Tell me about a time a customer need, product requirement, or technical constraint changed significantly after work was already underway. How did you reassess the plan, decide what to preserve or discard, and keep the people involved aligned?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. What new evidence changed your view, and how did you separate a necessary pivot from avoidable churn?
2. Which tradeoff or scope decision did you personally make after the change?
3. How did you communicate the impact on delivery, risk, and prior commitments?

## BEH-010 — Learn an Unfamiliar AI Technology

### Main Question

Tell me about a time you had to evaluate or learn an unfamiliar AI technology, model, framework, or technical approach quickly enough to make a real product or engineering decision. How did you learn, what did you test, and how did you decide whether to adopt it?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. How did you distinguish vendor claims or novelty from evidence relevant to the actual user problem?
2. What small experiment, comparison, or success criterion most influenced your decision?
3. What limitation or risk did you discover, and how did you communicate it to others?

## BEH-011 — Resolve a Technical Disagreement

### Main Question

Tell me about a meaningful technical disagreement with another engineer, product partner, or customer team. How did you understand the competing concerns, use evidence to reach a decision, and work with the group afterward?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. What part of the other person's position was valid, and did it change your own view?
2. How did you prevent authority, personality, or urgency from replacing technical evidence?
3. If the final decision differed from your recommendation, how did you support it and monitor the risk?

## BEH-012 — Translate Customer Ambiguity

### Main Question

Tell me about a time a customer or stakeholder brought you an important but ambiguous problem. How did you discover the real need, turn it into an executable technical scope, and confirm that the result delivered the intended value?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. Which assumption or requested feature changed after you investigated the underlying workflow?
2. How did you set boundaries when the customer wanted more than could safely or reliably be delivered?
3. What evidence did you use to confirm success after delivery?

## BEH-013 — Say No to an AI Feature

### Main Question

Tell me about a time you challenged or declined an appealing AI feature because the user value, feasibility, reliability, or risk did not justify building it as proposed. How did you evaluate the idea, communicate your position, and help find a better path?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. What evidence distinguished a real product concern from personal technical preference?
2. How did you preserve the underlying customer goal while changing or rejecting the requested feature?
3. What would have needed to change for you to support the original idea?

## BEH-014 — Discover a Security Risk

### Main Question

Tell me about a time you discovered a meaningful security, privacy, or authorization risk in a system. What did you do first, how did you communicate and escalate it, and how did you help drive the issue through containment and durable prevention?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. How did you assess potential exposure while avoiding further disclosure of sensitive information?
2. Who needed to know, and how did you balance urgency with accurate communication?
3. What evidence showed that remediation addressed the root cause and not only the observed symptom?

## BEH-015 — Recover a Slipping Project

### Main Question

Tell me about a project or customer commitment that was materially slipping. How did you determine what was actually wrong, take ownership of a recovery plan, and realign scope, people, and stakeholders?

### Likely Follow-Ups

1. Which work did you stop, defer, or simplify, and how did you protect the most important outcome?
2. How did you communicate the revised forecast and uncertainty without losing trust?
3. What lasting process or technical change reduced the chance of the same problem recurring?
